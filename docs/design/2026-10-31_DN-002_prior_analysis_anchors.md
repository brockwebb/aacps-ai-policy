# DN-002: prior analysis as anchors, and the corpus it implies

**Date:** 2026-10-31. **Source:** the operator's earlier review thread (claude.ai, October 2026), pasted
into the factory session. Recorded here so the graph build does not redo it. These are anchors in the
squiddy DN-028 sense: things the corpus must be able to confirm or refute, not questions it is built to
answer.

## Frame the corpus must carry

1. Policy DM is a compliance document on a statutory clock. The Maryland AI Ready Schools Act
   (HB1057 / SB720, 2026 session, effective 2026-06-01) requires each local system to adopt policy, align
   tool acquisition with MSDE guidance and appoint an AI coordinator; the fiscal note gives 120 days from
   MSDE's guidance release (June 2026). The draft's core sentence ("enhance, and never replace, the
   professional judgment of employees") tracks MSDE's vision statement nearly verbatim.
2. Maryland board policies are deliberately thin; the operative rules live in the administrative
   regulation. DM names DM-RA as a Related Entry and DM-RA is not published (verified 2026-10-31, see
   corpus/candidates/aacps-policy-dm_related_entries.yaml). The valid critique is what the policy fails to
   fix that a regulation cannot later fix, and adoption of DM ahead of a public DM-RA.

## Anchors (the eight findings, ranked for public comment)

| id | anchor | documents that confirm or refute it |
|----|--------|-------------------------------------|
| A1 | No definition of AI, while section B says AI is embedded in everyday tools; C.2 (approved purposes only) and C.6 (vetting before adoption) then either cover the whole existing tool stack or silently exempt it | DM; the Act's statutory definition; MSDE guidance; DI, DI-RA (technology use) |
| A2 | "Approved" has no approver, criteria or list; the Act contemplates an AI coordinator and approved tools | DM C.2; the Act; MSDE 05 responsibilities |
| A3 | C.1's operative terms "consequential decision" and "documented human oversight" are undefined | DM C.1; MSDE guidance; DL, DL-RA |
| A4 | C.1 gives "employees and stakeholders" authority to override or reject AI outputs; B lists students as stakeholders | DM B, C.1 |
| A5 | C.7 promises to say "when opt-out options apply" but creates no opt-out right; PPRA (cited) gives one for some collection; silent on school consent under COPPA | DM C.7; PPRA; COPPA; Md. Educ. 4-131 (Student Data Privacy Act); DL-RA |
| A6 | C.3 bias monitoring requires protected-class outcome data, in tension with data minimization; no direction | DM C.3; DL, DL-RA; Md. Educ. 4-131; AC |
| A7 | B names academic integrity and over-reliance as risks; C addresses neither beyond professional learning; student AI use deferred to C.4 | DM B, C.4; MSDE guidance |
| A8 | Drafting: "biased, discrimination, or inequitable"; "never replace" vs "may not replace"; no review clause though MSDE guidance is revised annually under the Act | DM; the Act |

Observed absence, not an anchor to refute: the draft has no internal contradictions; its failure mode is
emptiness. The draft makes almost no empirical claims, so there is little to cite against.

## Comment shape the anchors support

(a) a definition of AI and a scope statement for embedded features consistent with the statute; (b)
"consequential decision" defined and minimum documentation set in the policy, not the regulation; (c) DM
not adopted until DM-RA is published for comment with it, or adopted with a mandatory 12-month review.
Deadline 2026-11-21, policycomments@aacps.org, comments on policy and regulation only.
