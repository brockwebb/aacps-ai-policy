# corpus

`manifest.yaml` is the inventory: every document considered, with stage and disposition, written by
squiddy once the graph is initialized. `inbox/` holds files acquired by hand through a browser when a
host blocks machine pulls; each gets a receipt in the manifest. Bulky binaries are not committed; the
manifest's url, sha256 and retrieval date are the provenance.

Seed list (to be verified and completed by AP-01):
- AACPS draft Policy DM, Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence (Oct 2026), and any accompanying
  regulation or administrative procedure
- The AACPS policies and regulations Policy DM cites (acceptable use, student data privacy, academic
  integrity, records)
- Maryland State Department of Education guidance on AI in schools
- Federal documents it leans on (FERPA, COPPA, CIPA; Department of Education AI guidance)
- Any public board meeting agenda or minutes where the draft was presented
